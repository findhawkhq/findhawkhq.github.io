What Zebra Education Actually Is

Zebra Education Inc. operates BestMyTest, an online test-preparation platform focused on two exams: the TOEFL and the IELTS. The company’s stated mission is plain and simple: “Helping you get the score you need on your first try.” That first-try framing is the whole pitch — retaking either exam costs money and time, so structured prep that prevents a retake is where the value lives.

The company’s origin story: its founder had to take the TOEFL to attend university in Vancouver, BC, and found preparation harder than it needed to be, with no single place combining practice questions, simulated tests, lessons, and vocabulary tools — so the platform was built to fill that gap.

Who It’s For — and Who Should Skip It

This section matters more than the feature list, because the best prep tool in the world is useless if it doesn’t match how you study.

It’s for you if: you’re a self-motivated studier who does well on your own schedule — late nights, commutes, lunch breaks. It’s a particularly natural fit if you’ve already taken the exam once and missed your target score, because the simulated tests and adaptive practice let you drill the specific sections that dragged you down. International students preparing for university admission, and professionals who need a qualifying IELTS or TOEFL score, are the core audience. If you like studying in short, frequent sessions rather than long classroom blocks, this format plays to that.

Skip it if: you need a live teacher or classroom accountability to stay on track. This is a self-study platform — no tutor checks your work or pushes you to finish the next module. If you know you learn best with real-time feedback on speaking and writing, a tutor or classroom course will serve you better than any question bank. Also skip it if you’re already consistently hitting your target score on free practice tests — at that point, paid prep is solving a problem you don’t have.

Key Features Worth Knowing

Real features, from the platform itself — not marketing fluff:

  • Simulated full-length tests: Practice under real test conditions with timing that matches the actual exam. This is the most valuable feature for most students — a simulated run is the cheapest way to experience test-day pressure before the real thing.
  • Interactive lessons: Structured lessons that walk you through strategies for each section. Good for filling knowledge gaps, like understanding what the speaking section actually rewards.
  • Vocabulary tools: Built-in vocabulary study aimed at the words these exams test. Academic vocabulary is one of the quietest score-killers on both exams.
  • Adaptive learning: The platform adjusts to your level, steering you toward weak spots instead of rehashing material you’ve already mastered. That saves real study hours.
  • Free practice tests before you pay: You can take a free TOEFL or IELTS practice test without a credit card. Do this first — your diagnostic score tells you more about whether you need paid prep than any review ever could.

Compare the cost of prep against the cost of retaking the exam — the exam fee, the lost weeks waiting for a new date, and the stress of a delayed application. If structured practice gets you to your target one attempt sooner, the prep has paid for itself many times over. That’s the whole case for paid test prep, and it’s a fair one.

But keep the other side of the ledger in view: if you’re disciplined, free resources exist — practice tests on official exam sites, free YouTube channels, study communities. Take the free diagnostic test on BestMyTest first. If your score is already close to your target, a few weeks of free study may be enough. If you’re far off, or retaking after a miss, paid structure starts making sense.

The Bottom Line

BestMyTest is a straightforward self-study platform for TOEFL and IELTS prep, built by a company whose co-founder has been through the exam himself. No miracles, no guaranteed scores — just practice questions, simulated tests, lessons, and vocabulary tools organized in one place. If you study well on your own and want structured prep without classroom prices, it’s worth a look. And since the diagnostic tests are free, there’s no cost to finding out whether it’s a fit before you spend a dollar.
